## One bank, many surfaces

Technical Core does not ask a language model to invent graded questions. Every practice instance comes from a reviewed concept, item family, immutable question version, immutable rubric version and validated parameter generator. Your attempt remains tied to those exact versions even if editors later publish a correction.

## Master concepts across variants

Seeing one familiar sentence is not mastery. The engine rotates standard, reversed, numerical, assumption-changed, applied-company, explain-simply and follow-up-chain variants. Evidence is aggregated under the concept, not the wording.

```callout
variant: warning
title: No premature rankings
md: During calibration you will see mastery labels and evidence confidence, not a percentile, employer prediction or false precision.
```

## Misconceptions change the next drill

Exact arithmetic, sign, unit and assumption errors can be detected deterministically. Qualitative grading may classify evidence only against an approved rubric and misconception registry. A possible low-confidence misconception does not reduce mastery until it repeats or receives human confirmation.

## Live audio has two modes

Simulation mode hides feedback while the interview runs. Coach mode may give one short structural cue without revealing the answer. Audio uses a short-lived browser credential and is not stored by the testing implementation; only the transcript you review and submit becomes attempt evidence.

You can edit transcription errors before submission. Webcam-derived emotion, attractiveness and personality inference are not part of the product.
